# Animation spec — Chapter I

Stillness is the default. Movement is information.

| Element | Frames | Loop | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bulb | 3 (bright, dim, bright) | 20 s | one flicker, 180 ms total |
| Rain on glass | 4 | 2.4 s | only where the bulb catches |
| Cigarette smoke | 4 | 3.2 s | stops entirely when she is about to say something true |
| Eyes | 2 | 4–6 s random | blink; look away while lying, meet you before truth |
| Hands | 1 move per node max | — | the move is the tell; thumb to ring when lying |
| Clock hand | 1 step | 1 s | always four minutes behind the room's real time |
| Body | none | — | she stands or sits; the stillness is the pressure |
| Background | none | — | it is a painting |

**Player hands.** On screen at the start of every room, resting on a surface,
scar visible on the back of the right hand. They do not move until you move.

**Debrief.** Screen to black. Each missed tell appears as a small painted
vignette, three seconds, no text. Then Insight, Craft and Edge. Then the room
closes.
