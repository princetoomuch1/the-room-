# THE ROOM — Chapter I: The Last Ledger
## Story bible. One page. Not in the game.

Dated 2026-11-08. Narrative sign-off: Prince K. Parmar (pending).
The game reveals this in fragments and never states it whole. Every line in the
game must be consistent with it. `tools/check_story_consistency.js` enforces the
named facts below.

**1. Who was there in 2001.** Five men at a winter posting below the Prokletije,
on the Albanian side: Hale, Daniel Voss, Marek Zielinski, Pieter De Vries, Emil
Hasanović. One civilian translator attached to them: **Ines Marchetti**, 32, from
Trieste. Hale loved her. Voss took the photograph of the other four in January;
Hale is in it, Voss is behind the camera.

**2. Who died, and how.** On the night of 11 February 2001, Marek ordered a
descent in the dark to make a rendezvous that did not matter. Hale stayed at the
post on the radio. On the ice, De Vries was roped nearest Ines and did not put his
hand out. Emil watched. Voss knew by morning. Hale was told she slipped.

**3. What four of them swore.** Voss, Marek, De Vries and Emil swore never to
speak of it and never to be in one room again. Hale was not asked, because Hale
was not on the slope. That is why Voss says Hale is "the only one of us who was
never owned." Three kept the oath for twenty-five years. Voss broke it in August.

**4. What the cartel is.** A consortium inside the Antwerp port since 2003, when
Marek brought them a route. Spada is its public face. Its decisions are made by a
man Spada calls **the Greek**, who is never seen. Voss built three shipping funds
under one registered address; two clear the consortium's cargo, the third is his
own money.

**5. Why Voss vanished.** In August Voss asked the consortium to let him keep the
third fund and leave. They refused. He asked Emil to record the truth about 2001.
In September he set every clock he could reach four minutes slow, put the
photograph on Anna's fridge, and paid six months on a flat in Borgerhout. On
28 October at 21:20 he left a locked office and disappeared, to force the
brothers into one room, draw the consortium out, and test Hale. The 240 million is
not stolen. It is waiting.

**6. The bookkeeper.** **Lukas Vermeulen**, 51, married, two children, a cat
called Boris. He wrote to the board in October and nobody acted. When the game
begins he is **missing**, not dead. In the Desk, Hale makes the bank look at the
file. A clerk's note follows, it names Lukas as the only possible leak, and the
Greek has him killed. He is found in the dock at Merksem between the Desk and
the Table. Whatever line extracts the file is the line that kills him.

**7. Meera Rao.** Voss's chief of staff for eleven years. She is the voice on the
phone in the prologue. She knows where Voss is. She is not on anyone's side but
his.

**8. What every ending resolves against.** The brothers covered up Ines's death.
The consortium killed Lukas. Voss used Hale as the instrument of a twenty-five-
year attempt to put it right. Hale chooses whether to be the instrument, the
witness, the wall, or the next Voss.
