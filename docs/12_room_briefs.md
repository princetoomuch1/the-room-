# Painter's briefs — Rooms 2 to 8

The Kitchen is the bar. Each room below has one named light source with radial
falloff, five named colours, a composition that puts the NPC's hands where the
player can read them, one wrong object, and a texture note. Brushwork is a
turbulence displacement on every painted group; nothing is axis-aligned and clean.

## 2. The Desk — Joost Beelen

**Light.** One green banker's lamp, low, the bulb under a brass shade, so the
light pools on the counter and nothing above waist height is lit. The rest of the
room is the grey of a bank at four in the afternoon.

**Palette.** Bone (the counter's worn laminate), oxide red (a stamp pad), arsenic
green (the lamp's cast), burnt sienna (the desk's varnished edge), one gold (a pen
nib).

**Composition.** Joost sits behind a counter that is too high for him, so the
player sees his hands and his forehead and nothing in between. A queue ticket
hangs from a red plastic dispenser, number 047. The ledger is open and one page
has been squared with a ruler.

**Wrong object.** A second chair, pulled out, empty, with a coat on it that is not
his.

**Texture.** Paper grain on the ledger, varnish worn through to pale wood on the
counter edge where forty years of forearms have rested.

## 3. The Table — Mieke Carpentier

**Light.** A window behind her, overcast Antwerp light, so she is lit from behind
and the player gets her shape, not her detail. No lamp.

**Palette.** Bone (the walls), indigo (her suit), burnt sienna (the table), arsenic
green (a dying plant on the sill), one gold (a fountain pen capped on the
document).

**Composition.** She sits with her hands folded, one on top of the other, on a
folder squared to the table edge. A glass of water with a ring of condensation,
untouched.

**Wrong object.** A chair at the head of the table, empty, turned slightly out.
Somebody left in a hurry.

**Texture.** Canvas weave through the whole frame, the overcast light laid on
thick where it hits the window, nothing on the ceiling at all.

## 4. The Deposit — Radu Petrescu

**Light.** A single bare bulb in the stairwell, yellow, swinging very slightly.
The flat's door is half open and the light spills past him into the hall.

**Palette.** Bone (the stairwell plaster), oxide red (the door), burnt sienna (a
cardboard box), indigo (night through the window behind him), one gold (a key on
a nail).

**Composition.** Radu stands in the doorway and does not invite the player in.
Behind him, a flat emptied in a hurry: one chair, a mattress without sheets, a
suitcase already closed.

**Wrong object.** A second cup on the floor beside the suitcase, clean and dry.

**Texture.** Plaster flaking at the doorframe, a water stain spreading on the
ceiling of the hall.

## 5. The Meeting — Hendrik Sluys and the board

**Light.** Overhead fluorescents, one of them flickering, so the room is evenly
lit and slightly sick. Monday's agenda is still on the whiteboard.

**Palette.** Bone (the walls), arsenic green (the fluorescent cast), indigo
(Sluys's tie), burnt sienna (the table), one gold (a watch on a wrist).

**Composition.** Sluys at the head, one hand flat on a document, the other holding
a pen he is not using. The chairs along the table hold men whose faces are not
lit; the player sees cuffs and hands.

**Wrong object.** A phone, face down, in the middle of the table. Not his.

**Texture.** Scuff marks arcing where chairs have been pushed back, a coffee ring
nobody has wiped.

## 6. The Fund — Gioacchino Spada

**Light.** A wall of glass with the city behind it, but the light is flat because
it is raining. One desk lamp, warm, contradicting the window.

**Palette.** Bone (the ceiling), indigo (the sky and the rain), burnt sienna (the
desk), oxide red (a chair), one gold (a pen stand).

**Composition.** Spada stands at the window with his back to the player, hands in
his pockets. The desk carries three folders, one open, and a glass of something
amber he has not touched.

**Wrong object.** A small hard-sided case under the desk, packed.

**Texture.** Condensation beading on the inside of the glass, and the room
reflected faintly back into the window.

## 7. The War Room — Marek, De Vries, Emil

**Light.** A single pendant lamp over a long table, throwing the shadows wrong:
each man's shadow falls toward the player, not away.

**Palette.** Bone (the map), oxide red (the thread between the pins), burnt sienna
(the table), indigo (the window, night, rain), one gold (the recorder's
housing).

**Composition.** Three men, not sitting together. Marek stands by the map. De
Vries sits with his hands on his knees. Emil sits furthest away with a blanket
over his legs. The map carries pins and string that match no case file.

**Wrong object.** A fourth chair at the table, empty, with a glass in front of it,
half full.

**Texture.** The map's paper cockling where the pins go through, rain on the glass,
heat haze rising off the pendant lamp.

## 8. The Mirror

**Light.** Hospital flat. No warmth and no shadow anywhere.

**Palette.** Grey, grey, one bone, one indigo, one arsenic green: a single line of
light under a door.

**Composition.** The player's own hands on a surface, the scar visible, and behind
them a mirror that shows no face. It shows the room they came from.

**Wrong object.** A clock on the wall, four minutes slow, second hand moving.

**Texture.** None. The Mirror is the only room with no brushwork, and that is the
point.
