# Marek Zielinski — Soul Sheet

**1.** Marek Zielinski, fifty-eight, grew up in Legnica in a garrison town his mother refused to leave; she called him Mareczek and he has not heard the word since 1996.

**2.** Nothing, on paper. He has never signed a document in his life. He brought a route to a consortium in 2003 and has been paid in favours ever since.

**3.** Birds, specifically the counting of them. He keeps a notebook of what crosses the garden at Kalmthout and the entries are meticulous and go back nine years, and no one has ever seen it.

**4.** His contradiction is that he believes he is loyal. He has been loyal to the pact and disloyal to every single person the pact cost, and he has held that line for twenty-five years without once asking whether the line was in the right place. He is the one who might break, and the room has to let him.

**5.** His wound, said once, late, never explained: "I was the one who told the three of you to go back down."

**6.** What he wants from Hale is absolution he will not ask for. Hale is the only one of the four who was never owned, and Marek needs to know whether that was character or luck, because if it was luck then nobody could have done better and he can live with himself.

**7.** Three lines only he would say: "We agreed never to be in one room again. Look where we are." — "He has been very loyal to me ever since." — "Daniel chose you because you are the only one of us who was never owned. Are you now?"

**8.** He notices everything Hale has done that week in order, and he notices that Hale came to this house last, and the second thing is the only one that surprises him.

**9.** He will never say Lena's name first. He will say her name if Hale makes him, and he will not say it twice.

**10. Clean:** he sets the lighter on the mantel and does not pick it up again, and he answers the questions, and he is the one who tells Emil to play the tape. **Partial:** he gives up De Vries to keep the route and knows precisely what he has done. **Dark:** he sells everyone, keeps the route, and spends the following year counting birds. **Refusal:** the door closes and the lighter starts turning again, and six weeks later he sends flowers to a funeral and they are the largest arrangement there.
